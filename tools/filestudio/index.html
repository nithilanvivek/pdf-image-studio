        Fine tune

        Options

        Every page is named in order and delivered together in one ZIP file.

        Content is fitted proportionally and centered. Extra space becomes blank margins on the sides or top and bottom.

        How page width is standardized

        The widest page becomes the target. Narrower pages are resized proportionally until their widths match it, so their heights change naturally and nothing is stretched or cropped.

        What this removes

        Embedded fonts, selectable text, editable objects, forms, links, annotations, attachments, scripts, and document metadata.

        What this preserves

        The visible page appearance, page proportions, and selected raster resolution. Pages are stored as lossless images and normalized to one common width.

        Important: text will no longer be selectable or searchable after flattening. Higher DPI produces a sharper but larger PDF.

          Compression level

          LZWLossless. Best for text, forms, diagrams, line art and mostly white pages.

          DeflateLossless. A good general alternative that can beat LZW on some documents.

          JPEGLossy. Usually much smaller for photos, scans, gradients and detailed imagery.

          Why can TIFF be large? TIFF stores rendered pixels instead of the PDF’s compact text and vector instructions. Compression, grayscale and lower DPI can reduce the result considerably.

          • Chrome or Edge: use Install app in the address bar or browser menu.
